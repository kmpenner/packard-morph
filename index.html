Packard Morphology Expander

Converts CCAT/Packard codes (e.g. packard:VAI-AMI2S) into readable labels. Paste one code per line, or paste TEI containing morph="packard:…" attributes.

CodeExpansion

Unrecognised letters are shown in [brackets]. The tables were compiled from the CCAT scheme and should be checked against the CCAT documentation, especially the less common voice letters (E, D, O, N).
